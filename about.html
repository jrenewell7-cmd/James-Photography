ABOUT

Hi, I'm James.


A photographer and videographer based in Sydney, Australia. My work focuses on sport, adventure and the moments that bring people together. From the intensity of competition to the excitement of travel and exploration, I aim to create imagery that captures authentic experiences and tells meaningful stories.


Having recently graduated high school, I'm now pursuing photography and videography more seriously, with the goal of continuing to develop my craft and share stories from Australia and around the world.
